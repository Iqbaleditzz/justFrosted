Our Story

Just Frosted was born from a simple belief — that the most meaningful cakes are made at home, with real ingredients and even realer love. Based in the heart of Calicut, we craft every cake by hand, from layered birthday cakes to delicate wedding tiers. No preservatives, no shortcuts — just fresh flavours and heartfelt baking. Whether you want something classic or completely custom, we bring your sweetest moments to life.
